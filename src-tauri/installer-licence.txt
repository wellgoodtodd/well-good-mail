Well Good Mail

Before you install

Well Good Mail is a free, early app from Well Good Media Ltd, shared as it is. It works with your email, so please keep your own backups of anything important. We can't promise it will be free of mistakes, or promise support, though we do read everything we are sent.

Your mail stays on your own computer. The app talks only to your mail provider (Google, Microsoft, Apple or your own mail server) and to GitHub to look for updates. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no tracking.
