# 0.7.3: what to check after the release

0.7.3 is beta 13 with the version number changed. Ticked in beta 13 (13 Nov): the Attachments setting and its preview, "Saved in PC Downloads", and Send To forwards without a signature. Everything below is still to see. The longer list of older items is in `testing-0.7.3-beta13.md`.

## Keep an eye on

- [ ] **The update itself:** the betas move to 0.7.3 by themselves (or from Settings › Updates), and What's New shows once.
- [ ] **Alerts for tasks** (kept until seen): a task with a time gives a notification at that time.
- [ ] **AI drafts from your EA** end at the sign-off with your signature once.
- [ ] **A Send To rule on its own:** the next SocialPilot invoice saves and forwards by itself, once, and shows in What's been sent.
- [ ] **Find my invoices** reads properly, with Stripe and PayPal receipts listed by the company you paid.
- [ ] **Show in folder** opens the file's own folder with the file selected.

## When there's time

- [ ] **Uninstall and reinstall:** the data box moves the two media.wellgood.mail folders to the Recycle Bin, and Welcome back adds the accounts again.
- [ ] **Pause for 1 hour** really quietens alerts for the hour.
- [ ] **Move an event to a calendar in another account** from the quick edit card.
