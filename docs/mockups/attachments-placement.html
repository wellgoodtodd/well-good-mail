Attachments: above or below, and how big

The real reading pane with a Zoom invoice and two files. A new card in Settings › Appearance (Reading) chooses where attachments sit and how big they are. Right-click works the same on every size.

Today's layout, kept as an option. With a long email you scroll to reach the files.

My suggestion

  • Default: Above · Medium. Files are in reach without scrolling, and big enough to right-click comfortably. Anyone who prefers today's look picks Below · Large.
  • Any mix works (Below · Small, Above · Large...). Pictures keep their thumbnails at every size, and Save all appears beside them when there are several.
  • Writing an email isn't affected: this is only for reading.
