Calendar: dragging, quick edit and moving several events

Day and Week view get handles and dragging, Month view gets dragging between days, the pop-up becomes a quick edit, and you can pick several events and move them to another calendar in one go. Edit opens the full New event form.

4 · One click: what should it do?

A · MY PICKClick opens the quick edit, as now

Ctrl-click or Shift-click picks events without opening anything, and dragging across empty space draws a box round several. One click to see or join a meeting, as it is today.

BClick selects, double-click opens (Apple Calendar)

A click outlines the event; double-click, Enter or right-click opens the quick edit. Tidier for picking several, but every look at an event, including Join, takes a double-click.
