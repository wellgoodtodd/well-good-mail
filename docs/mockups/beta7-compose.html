What changes

1. Font and size sit in neat boxes with small up and down arrows, the same height as the other buttons. Click to pick from the list as now; the arrows step to the next font or size.

2. A clear "on" look for bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, lists and quote: the button gets the same soft blue box as a selected sidebar item, not a bright fill. Thin dividers split the toolbar into font, style, colour, lists, and the rest.

3. Attach and Emoji as labelled icons next to Send (an outline icon with the word under it). Attach leaves the toolbar and the bottom row, so there's one place for it; dragging files onto the email still works.

4. Emoji opens the Windows emoji panel (the same as Win + .), and the emoji goes where the cursor is. Nothing of our own to keep up to date.

5. Attachments become cards two to a row: a picture's thumbnail, or a coloured file-type tile, with the name and size; × shows when you hover. A total sits above them. Big files that go as a link keep their note.
