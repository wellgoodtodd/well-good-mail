What's new in this version
  • Search settings at the top of the list, and the account needing attention is flagged there (Sign in).
  • Ask: type what you want; it shows the change and nothing happens until you click Apply.
  • Health line: one sentence on how the account is doing, with a fix button when something's wrong.
  • Suggestions: one at a time, one click to accept or dismiss.
  • Live preview of a real email with the signature in it.
  • Saves as you go, with Undo. No Save buttons.
  • New rows: Sending as (aliases) and Notifications per account, including quiet hours.
  • On a wide window the editor sits in a third column to the right, like Apple's System Settings.
