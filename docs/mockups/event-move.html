Invitations from other people stay where they arrived, as the organiser owns them. You can still answer them from the invitation email.

No copying and pasting needed: picking another account's calendar on a repeating event copies the series there with the same repeat (every week, every other Monday, the 1st of each month and so on). Past ones stay where they were. With the switch on (the usual), the original series stops, so it's a move; switch it off to keep both. Within the same account, changing the calendar moves just that one, as now. A repeat Outlook or Google can't take (rare) says so instead.
