3 · Out of Office when you're away from your computer

Yesterday Claude asked, but the question only appears in the app on your desktop, so it waited. Out of Office reaches other people, so it's set to always ask, with Allow greyed out. The idea: a second choice, off by default, where you decide the rules once, and Claude can switch it on within them from anywhere.

SettingsAI Connection › Set Out of Office

Ask me in the app each timeHow it works now. Needs you at the computer.
Let Claude set it, within these limitsIt can turn Out of Office on from a chat. It can't go beyond what's below.
Who gets the reply: Contacts only ▾Claude can't make it wider than this.
Longest it can be on: 3 days ▾It always needs a back date inside this.
Message: Claude writes it, shows me first ▾Or: only my saved templates.
Accounts: the ones set to Read and write above.

When you're back at the computer there's a note with Turn off, and it's in the AI log (Recent) too. Claude is told to show you the accounts, dates, who and the whole message and wait for your yes before it sets anything. The app itself holds the limits, so a mistake or a trick in an email can't send it to everyone or turn it on for a month.

Not on your phone: Well Good Mail is only the Windows app, so this is the way to reach it from a chat. As far as I know the Gmail phone app can't set Out of Office at all, so the web or this is the way.
