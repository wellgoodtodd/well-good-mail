Send To: forwarding and filing attachments

From the real attachment card in the reading pane. Four parts: the right-click menu, what happens after a send (with "Do this every time?"), the places and rules in Settings, and Find my invoices. Example addresses here are made up.

1. Right-click an attachment

Forward opens the normal writing window as a forward with only this file, from your usual sender. Send to lists your saved places.

The same Forward and Send to appear when you right-click an email in the list, and when several are selected: search "invoice", select them all, Send to › Bookkeeping, and each email goes as its own forward with its files.

Places are a short list you set up once. The top one can be the default, like lists in Tasks: ☆ in Settings.

To agree

  • Name: "Send to" in menus and "Send To" in Settings, with "places" for the saved list. Or "Forward to" if you prefer that wording.
  • Sending: a place sends straight away with Undo (shown), unless you pick "Open the writing window first" for it.
  • What goes: only the attachments by default (Hubdoc reads the PDF), with a tick to send the email's text too.
  • Rules only run while Well Good Mail is open, catching up when it starts. Gmail's own filters could forward with the app closed, but only for Gmail, and the address has to be confirmed in Gmail first.
  • Later: asking an AI app to "send this year's subscription invoices to Bookkeeping", with an approval card.
