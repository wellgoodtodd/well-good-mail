New: invitations you haven't answered, soonest first, with a note if one clashes with something already in your calendar. Pressing Yes, Maybe or No answers it and it moves to Replied (with Undo). Cancelled meetings show crossed out with Remove from my calendar. Replied: upcoming ones you've answered, your answer coloured (green Yes, amber Maybe, red No), so changing your mind is one click. › opens the usual event pop-up, and the email it came in is a click from there.

Each answer asks the organiser's calendar to tell them (Google and Outlook do this). Past events drop off the Replied list.
