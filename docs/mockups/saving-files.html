Clear list

Like Chrome's downloads: the button only shows in the toolbar once you've saved something, with a dot after a new save. It lists the last 20 files you saved from the app, wherever they went, with Open and Show in folder. Files that have since been moved or deleted say "Moved or deleted". The list is cleared after 7 days or with Clear list.
