Calendar Groups apply to tasks too. The All / Work / Personal switch is the same one the calendar uses, and picking a group shows only the task lists of the accounts in that group (here Work, so Home and Shopping are hidden). Lists still sit under their account's name, with the kind of list beside it (Google Tasks or Microsoft To Do). Today, Scheduled and All Tasks look across every list in the group, like Apple Reminders' smart lists. The ★ marks the group's default list.

Ticking off: click the circle and the task stays where it is, crossed out with the time you did it, for the rest of the day, so you can see what you've got through. Next morning it's put away under Completed before today, still there if you want to look back. Ctrl+Z (or Undo in the toast) brings a task back. Ticking off works both ways: done on the phone shows done here, and the other way round. Anything not done by its day turns red under Overdue. Coming up stays on the right, as it does in List and Calendar.

+Order more gaffer tape next Tuesday #studio

The words it understood are highlighted in the text, and the chip underneath says what it made of them. They're taken out of the title when the task is saved, so this one is called "Ring the dentist". Click ✕ on the chip to keep the words as typed instead.

"Friday" on a Friday means next Friday, as people usually mean it. "Today 3pm" is there for this afternoon.

The list is the one you're looking at (or the default list when you're in Today or All Tasks). Click the chip to change it, or type #studio, #home and so on.

It reads the same words New event already understands: today, tomorrow, weekdays, next week, 3pm, 15:00, 20 Nov, in 2 weeks, every Monday.

Right-click › Make a task in the message list or the message view, or press T. The subject becomes the title (with "Re:" and "Fwd:" taken off) and you can change it before it's saved. If the email mentions a date ("by end of day today"), the due date is filled in with it, highlighted so you can see where it came from.

In the list the task carries a small ✉ subject link. Opening the task shows From: <subject> at the top; clicking it opens the email, in any folder, even after it's archived. The link is saved in the task's notes too, so Google Tasks and To Do on the phone show a link back to the email in Gmail or Outlook.

⚠ One more sign-in per account, once

Google: Well Good Mail will need the Google Tasks permission, which it hasn't asked for until now. After the update, each Google account signs in once more (one time only). Until it does, its lists show "Sign in again to see tasks" and nothing else changes: mail and calendar carry on as normal. The Google Tasks API also needs turning on in Todd's Cloud project, so it goes into SETUP.md and the first-run guide.

Microsoft: Microsoft To Do needs the Tasks.ReadWrite permission, so each Microsoft 365 account signs in once more in the same way.

Being honest about times: Google Tasks keeps a due date over its API but not a due time. A time set here (like 14:00 for the deposit) lives only in Well Good Mail, and is also written at the top of the task's notes ("Due 14:00") so it isn't lost on the phone. The Google Tasks app shows the date only, and its own reminder can't use our time. Microsoft To Do keeps both date and time, so there's no catch there. iCloud and other IMAP accounts have no tasks.
