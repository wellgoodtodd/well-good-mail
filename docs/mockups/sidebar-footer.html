How it would work

Same behaviour as now: click a card to go there; counts follow the group you're looking at (Scheduled would gain a count of emails waiting to send). The page you're on is shaded blue.

Drag to reorder the four cards, like the Tasks sidebar's All Tasks, Today and Scheduled.

Narrow sidebar: below about 200px wide the cards fall back to option B's icon row, so nothing gets squashed.

Size: a small job (one part of the sidebar, plus its styles), so it could go in beta 8, or wait for the release after.
