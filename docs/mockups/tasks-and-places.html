Quick add reads the date from what you type, like New event does. From an email: right-click › Make a task, or press T: the subject becomes the title, and the task links back to the email. Ticking the task off can also archive the email (a switch, off by default).

AI apps could add tasks too ("make a task to reply to Glen on Friday"), asking first like everything else.

Due times show as a dashed block in Day and Week view, and on the day in Month view, with a tick box. Ticking it off there marks it done.

MY PICKYour own places first, map places optional

Used before and from your contacts need nothing new and never leave your computer. That covers most of what you type.

Places (map search) needs a maps service: Google Places or Microsoft (Azure) Maps. Both need a key and charge past a free monthly allowance, and what you type goes to them. I'd add it as a switch, off by default.

Either way, events with a place get Directions in their pop-up, which opens Google Maps (or Apple Maps on a phone link) in the browser.
