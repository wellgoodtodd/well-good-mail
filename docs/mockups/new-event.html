Repeat has the same choices as Apple. Custom means every N days, weeks, months or years, on chosen weekdays, or "the 2nd Tuesday". End repeat can be Never, On date or After N times.

Alerts: None, At time of event, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, 1 or 2 days, or 1 week before. Up to two alerts. All-day events get "on the day at 09:00" and "the day before at 09:00".

Video call offers Google Meet on Google accounts and Teams on Microsoft 365. iCloud and IMAP calendars have no Video call row.

Invitees are suggested from your contacts. The button then reads "Add and send invitations", and the account itself (Google or Outlook) emails them, the same as on the phone.
