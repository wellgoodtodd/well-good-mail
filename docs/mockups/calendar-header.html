Ctrl + mouse wheel (or pinch on a trackpad) zooms in and out of the times, and the small slider does the same for anyone who doesn't know that. Zoomed out, you see the whole day. Zoomed in, the hours grow and the lines show half hours, then quarter hours, then 5 minutes. The zoom is remembered.

Snapping follows the zoom: 15 minutes normally, 5 minutes at the closest zoom. An event that starts off the grid, say at 10:05, keeps its 5 minutes past as you drag it (10:05, 10:20, 10:35), rather than jumping to 10:15.
